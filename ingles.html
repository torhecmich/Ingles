Wedding Passport Itinerary
📍 Entry Stamp – Mexico City 🇲🇽

May 15 – Get ready for the adventure!

On day one, we’ll have transportation waiting for you at the Mexico City airport to take you straight to a truly Mexican experience: Las Trajineras de Xochimilco!

Jenna and Hector will be together at the airport to welcome you and group up the different flights arriving for the wedding.

Once everyone’s together, a bus will pick us up and take us to our floating celebration.

You’ll spot us easily—we’ll be holding a pink flag 🎀, so you can’t miss us!

We’ve got the food covered: we’ll be serving tostadas de tinga de pollo, a delicious homemade Mexican dish 🌮❤️

Drinks, souvenirs, and live music are on you, so make sure to bring some cash in pesos 💵 in case you want to request a mariachi song, grab a michelada, or buy a little keepsake 🎶🍹🎁

💰 We recommend exchanging your USD to pesos at the airport upon arrival for better convenience during the ride!

Just bring your best vibes for a ride full of amazing food, music, laughter, and unforgettable memories.

After the boat ride, the same transportation will take you to the beautiful Hacienda Cocoyoc, where you can rest and unwind.

We’ll arrive around 7:30 PM, and the on-site restaurant will be open until 10:00 PM, just in case you want a light dinner before bed. 🍽️✨
🛂 Relax Visa – Hacienda Cocoyoc 🌺

May 16 – Day 2: Relax, Love & Celebration

After the Trajinera party, Friday is all about enjoying the beautiful Hacienda Cocoyoc at your own pace.

Feel free to explore the gardens, relax by the pool, visit the spa, or simply rest. 🌿💆‍♂️🌞

Breakfast and lunch are on your own, so treat yourself to whatever sounds good that day. ☕🍽️

At 5:00 PM, a bus will pick up anyone who wants to join us for our Mexican civil wedding ceremony 💍

It’ll be a special moment, followed by a fun icebreaker to help everyone get to know each other and kick off the celebration.

Dinner will be on us, but we kindly ask that you let us know in advance if you’ll be joining, so we can plan accordingly. Find the RSVP Link below. 🙏💌

We expect to return to the hotel around 10:00 PM, so everyone can rest up for the big day: the wedding! 💒✨
🎯 Ceremony Clearance – LoveLand 💒

May 17 – The Big Day

The big day is here!

Transportation to the ceremony venue will be ready to depart at 3:15 PM sharp, as the ceremony starts at 4:00 PM.

Out of respect for the schedule and other guests, the bus will leave on time, so if you miss it, you’ll need to take a taxi on your own.

The party officially ends at 4:00 AM, and the last bus will depart at that time with the true dance floor champions. 💃🕺
